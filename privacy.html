Summary

GodEye is an offline-first security suite. We do NOT collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. No photos, videos, audio, face data, thumb data, or GPS location are uploaded to any server by us. Everything stays on your device unless YOU choose to share / export it. If you tap GPS Share, YOU share location. If you export PDF, YOU export it. If you share on WhatsApp, YOU share it.

Data Collection

GodEye does NOT collect, store, or share any personal data on our servers. No account is required. No name, email, phone number, or address is asked for by us. Any shop / office credentials you enter (Shop Name, Phone, Address) stay on your phone only, and only show on export if you choose Show Credentials.

Permissions We Use & Why

  • Camera: For live camera view, photo capture, video recording, torch, barcode scan, document scan, face detection (468 Dots), counting, thumb biometric. Used only on your device.
  • Microphone: For voice commands (Capture, Bill, Scan, Torch, GPS Share, etc). Voice is processed on your device / by your phone's speech service. We do not record or upload audio.
  • Location - GPS: For GPS Timestamp + GPS Share + Anti-Fake GPS only. When you tap Get GPS / GPS Share / Capture / Attendance. GPS is NOT tracked in background. GPS is NOT uploaded to server. You can deny Location permission or turn GPS OFF anytime in GPS Settings. If you deny Location, GPS Timestamp / GPS Share will not work, but Camera / Bill / Scan / Face / Thumb still work. City Only mode available for privacy - Shows Wana only, no exact coordinates.
  • Storage / Photos: To save photos, videos, PDFs and bills you choose to export, on your device only.
  • Bluetooth: For External Devices PRO - Bluetooth Printer / Bluetooth Devices - PRO Only. Used only when you tap Bluetooth PRO.
  • USB OTG: For USB Cam / USB Storage / UVC Microscope / Endoscope. Used only when you connect USB device.
  • Internet / WiFi: For WiFi Microscope + IP Camera only. Not for uploading your data.
  • Notifications: For Unknown face alert only.

Camera & Microphone

Camera and Microphone are used ONLY on your device for live viewing, torch, scanning, face, thumb, and voice commands. All processing happens on your phone. You can deny these permissions anytime from Settings > Apps > GodEye > Permissions.

Face Data

Face detection (468 Dots) is for on-device alerts and logs only. Face tags you create are stored only in your phone's local storage. No face images or biometric data are uploaded, shared, or used for identification by us. Get consent from person before enrolling their face.

Thumb Biometric Data

Thumb Biometric (NADRA-Style) pattern is created on your phone, stored only on your phone, never uploaded, never sent to server, like Face. Thumb is used for Thumb Enroll / Verify / Attendance only. Get consent from person before enrolling their thumb. If you uninstall app, thumb patterns are deleted permanently. We cannot recover them.

GPS Location Data

GPS Timestamp: When you tap Get GPS / GPS Share / Capture / Attendance, app gets location from phone GPS. Location stays on phone, only shows on photo / export if YOU share / export it. Location is NOT tracked in background. Location is NOT uploaded to server by us. You can turn GPS OFF anytime. If you share GPS photo on WhatsApp, receiver will see location + map link - That is YOUR action, not ours. Choose City Only mode if you want privacy - Shows Wana only, no exact GPS coordinates.

Anti-Fake GPS: App checks if GPS is fake (isFromMockProvider / GPS Jump / Fake GPS App). If GPS is fake, Attendance / GPS Share will be blocked or marked as Fake GPS - No Proof. Fake GPS attempts are logged on your phone only, for owner to see. This is to protect shop / office owner from fake attendance / fake delivery proof.

Credentials - Shop / Office / Department

Universal Credentials (Shop / Office / Department / School / Hospital / Factory / Personal) you enter stay on your phone only. They only show on export / share if you choose Show Credentials. You are responsible for credentials you enter. Check before export / share.

Local Storage

All settings, bills, scans, counts, history, face, thumb, GPS logs, attendance, effects are stored only on your device. You can clear them anytime from App > Clear Data, or Settings > Apps > GodEye > Clear Data. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data - Including Face, Thumb, GPS, Bills, Scans.

PRO & Watermark

FREE exports show Made By WanaLab watermark. PRO removes watermark. No SHOP PRO, No HARDWARE BUNDLE - Only one PRO: Rs. 2,500 / Year.

Third Party Services

GodEye does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or trackers. The app loads open-source libraries (TensorFlow, MediaPipe Face Mesh, Tesseract OCR) for on-device AI. No data is sent to those libraries. If you tap Map Link, it opens Google Maps - That is your tap, not ours. If you share via WhatsApp / Gmail, that share is your action, not ours.

Children

GodEye is not directed to children under 13, and does not knowingly collect data from children. Parents / teachers should supervise microscope / camera / thumb / GPS use. Do not enroll child's thumb / face without parent consent.

Data Deletion

Since no data leaves your device, there is nothing to delete on our servers. To delete all app data, uninstall the app or use Clear Data. For help, email Nasir@wanalab.org
