Privacy Policy - God Eye

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Made by WanaLab

Summary

God Eye is an offline-first security suite. We do NOT collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. No photos, videos, audio, face data, or location are uploaded to any server. Everything stays on your device.

Data Collection

God Eye does NOT collect, store, or share any personal data. No account is required. No name, email, phone number, or address is asked for.

Permissions We Use & Why

  • Camera: For live camera view, photo capture, video recording, torch, barcode scan, document scan, face detection, and counting. Used only on your device.
  • Microphone: For voice commands (Capture, Bill, Scan, Torch, etc). Voice is processed on your device / by your phone's speech service. We do not record or upload audio.
  • Storage / Photos: To save photos, videos, PDFs and bills you choose to export, on your device only.

Camera & Microphone

Camera and Microphone are used ONLY on your device for live viewing, torch, scanning, and voice commands. All processing happens on your phone. You can deny these permissions anytime from Settings > Apps > God Eye > Permissions.

Face Data

Face detection is for on-device alerts and logs only. Face tags you create are stored only in your phone's local storage. No face images or biometric data are uploaded, shared, or used for identification by us.

Local Storage

All settings, bills, scans, counts, and history are stored only on your device. You can clear them anytime from App > Clear Data, or Settings > Apps > God Eye > Clear Data. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.

Third Party Services

God Eye does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or trackers. The app loads open-source libraries (TensorFlow, Tesseract OCR) for on-device AI. No data is sent to those libraries.

Children

God Eye is not directed to children under 13, and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Data Deletion

Since no data leaves your device, there is nothing to delete on our servers. To delete all app data, uninstall the app or use Clear Data.

Changes To This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and update the policy in the Play Store listing.

Contact

For questions, contact WanaLab via the Play Store listing for God Eye.
