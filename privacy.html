Privacy Policy - God Eye

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Made by WanaLab

Data Collection

God Eye does NOT collect, store, or share any personal data. No photos, videos, audio, or location are uploaded to any server.

Camera & Microphone

Camera and Microphone are used ONLY on your device for live viewing, torch, scanning, and voice commands. All processing happens on your phone.

Local Storage

All settings and history are stored only on your device. You can clear them anytime from Settings > Apps > God Eye > Clear Data.

Third Party Services

God Eye does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or trackers.

Contact

For questions, contact WanaLab via Play Store listing.
