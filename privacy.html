Offline-First

GodEye works offline. Your photos, face data, thumb data, GPS, and documents stay on your device by default. We do not auto-upload them.

User-Initiated Sending Only

Shop and Export pages have MQTT / WebSocket / HTTP sending features. These run ONLY when YOU tap Send / Share / Export. We never send your photos, thumb, face, phone, or ID/license without your explicit tap and consent.

GPS

GPS is OFF by default for Personal use. GPS only turns on when you tap Enable GPS. GPS is used only for timestamp / GPS Share if you enable it.

What We Don't Collect

We do not collect your photos, biometrics, or documents on our servers unless you explicitly choose to share/export them.
