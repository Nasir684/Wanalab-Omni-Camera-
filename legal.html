GPS - How It Works - Privacy

GPS is OFF by default for Personal Profile. GPS is ON by default for Shop / Office / Department Profile. You can turn GPS OFF in GPS Settings. When GPS is ON, app gets location from phone GPS only when you tap Get GPS / GPS Share / Capture / Attendance. Location is NOT tracked in background. Location is NOT uploaded. Location only appears on photo / export if you share / export. If you share GPS photo on WhatsApp, receiver will see location + map link - That is YOUR action, not ours. Choose City Only mode if you want privacy - Shows Wana only, no exact coordinates.

Thumb / Face - How It Works - Privacy

Thumb pattern and Face 468 Dots pattern are created on your phone, stored on your phone, never sent to server. No one else can see your thumb / face data unless you export it. For Thumb enrollment, get consent from person.
